Privacy Policy

MyMoney Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 10, 2026

MyMoney ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the MyMoney mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

When you create an account or sign in, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Authentication-related identifiers

b. Financial and User Content

When you use the App, we may collect and store:

  • Transactions you create
  • Budget data
  • Categories
  • Notes you enter
  • Workspace-related information
  • Receipt or attachment images that you upload

c. Device and Local Storage Data

The App may store data locally on your device to support offline use, faster access, and synchronization.

d. Imported and Exported Data

If you choose to import or export data, the App may process files selected by you, such as CSV or JSON files.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the core features of the App
  • Save and organize your financial records
  • Sync your data across sessions or devices
  • Store receipt attachments
  • Improve reliability and security of the App
  • Respond to support requests

3. Cloud Services and Third-Party Processing

The App uses third-party infrastructure to operate certain features.

Supabase

We use Supabase for services such as:

  • User authentication
  • Cloud database storage
  • File storage for uploaded attachments
  • Workspace and synchronization features

Your information may be transmitted to and stored on Supabase infrastructure as necessary to provide these services.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage

  • Locally on your device
  • In cloud storage or database services used by the App

Some data may remain on your device after sign-out so it can be available again when you sign in and sync.

5. Receipt Images and Attachments

If you choose to upload receipt or attachment images, those files may be stored in cloud storage associated with the App in order to let you access them later.

You control whether to upload such files.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share data only in these limited situations:

  • With service providers required to operate the App, such as Supabase
  • If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the App

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide the App's services and fulfill legitimate business or legal purposes.

Locally stored data may remain on your device until you remove the App data or uninstall the App, subject to your device behavior and platform rules.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your data.

You may also:

  • Stop using the App at any time
  • Delete local app data through your device settings
  • Contact us to request account or cloud data deletion

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. International Users

If you use the App from outside the country where the service infrastructure is located, your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.
