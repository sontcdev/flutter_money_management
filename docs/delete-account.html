Account Deletion

Delete your MyMoney account

App: MyMoney · Developer: tcson.dev

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in and open Settings → Delete account.
  2. If shared workspaces are listed, open each workspace and leave it first. Transfer ownership before leaving a workspace you own. Pending memberships and archived shared workspaces can also prevent deletion.
  3. Read the confirmation, type DELETE, and confirm deletion.
  4. Keep the app open while deletion completes. On success, you are signed out and returned to sign-in. Your deleted account can no longer sign in.

If deletion fails, the app shows an error and lets you retry. Do not assume deletion is complete until the app confirms success.

Request deletion without the app

  1. Send an email to tcson.dev@gmail.com with the subject "MyMoney account deletion request".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to MyMoney, or include that address in the message.
  3. We will verify account ownership before processing the request. Shared workspace membership or ownership must be resolved first. Do not send your password or sign-in codes.

Contact us if you cannot access your account or leave a shared workspace.

Data that will be deleted

  • Your account (email, display name, sign-in credentials)
  • Your sole-member workspaces and their wallets, categories, transactions, budgets, recurring transactions, attachments and activity logs
  • Your membership and preferences in workspaces owned by other users
  • Your notifications
  • Your uploaded receipt files and account-linked error reports

Data that may be retained

Records you contributed to shared workspaces may remain available to other members after you leave and delete your account. Your account's author identifiers are removed from retained records, but information you entered in transaction notes or other shared content may remain. Resolve shared content with the workspace owner before leaving. Backup retention depends on the hosting provider's configured retention schedule; a fixed backup deletion period is not promised here.
